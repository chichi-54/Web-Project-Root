📚 Effective Study Methods Guide

Discover proven techniques to help you study smarter, boost memory, and improve academic results.

1️⃣ The Feynman Technique

Purpose: Explain concepts in simple terms to deeply understand them.

  • Explain the topic as if teaching a child.
  • Identify gaps in understanding.
  • Study again and simplify further.

Best For: Math, Science, and complex concepts.

2️⃣ Active Recall

Purpose: Strengthen memory by retrieving information without notes.

  • Study → close notes → write everything remembered.
  • Identify what you missed and repeat.

Tools: Flashcards, practice quizzes.

3️⃣ Spaced Repetition

Purpose: Boost long‑term retention through spaced review sessions.

Review schedule:

  • Day 1 – Learn
  • Day 2 – Review
  • Day 4 – Review
  • Day 7 – Review
  • Day 14 – Review

4️⃣ Pomodoro Technique

Purpose: Maintain focus using timed intervals.

  • 25 minutes focused work
  • 5 minute break
  • Longer break after 4 cycles

Best For: Staying focused and avoiding burnout.

5️⃣ Cornell Note‑Taking Method

Purpose: Organize notes for maximum retention.

  • Right column → Notes
  • Left column → Questions
  • Bottom → Summary

6️⃣ Mind Mapping

Purpose: Visual learning through connected concepts.

  • Central idea in the middle
  • Branches for subtopics
  • Use colors, arrows & images
